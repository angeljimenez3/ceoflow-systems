## 1. Tier 11 (tiereleven.com)
- **H1:** *"WE FIND WHAT'S HOLDING YOU BACK. THEN WE FIX IT."*
- **Sub:** Strategy first. Then execution. Across every channel.
- **CTA:** "Tell us what's not working" → `/apply`
- **Above-fold proof:** Logo strip — Lawn Doctor, Mindvalley, AirSculpt, Pet Meds, Milk Street, Credit Repair Cloud. One testimonial: "scaled our ads from $20K/mo to over $200K/mo."
- **Colors:** `#ff6496` hot pink accent, `#f4f4f4` light grey, `#7958d2` violet, `#222222` text, `#fed61b` yellow.
- **Fonts:** Inter (workhorse), Roboto, Lato.
- **Vibe:** Light, editorial, premium-consultative. McKinsey deck with hot-pink callouts.
- **Best at:** "Diagnose before we prescribe" — refuses to sell ads, sells diagnosis.
- **Weakness:** No hard numbers above fold. No "$X managed", no client count.

## Patterns of the Top 10%

The five things every elite paid-ads agency site does:

### 1. One typeface, used with confidence
Acquisition.com = all Poppins. Disruptive = Antonio + Inter. Tier 11 = Inter. Common Thread = Poppins + Inter. Nobody loads four fonts. **Restraint = premium.**

### 2. Hero = problem statement, not service description
None say "We run Facebook ads." They say "Most budgets are wasted" / "We find what's holding you back" / "Do you want to scale?" The headline accuses a pain — the buyer pre-qualifies themselves.

### 3. One accent color owns the brand
Tier 11 = hot pink. Acquisition = electric purple. Disruptive = red. CTC = lime. KlientBoost = cyan. **One signature hue, used everywhere.** CFS already has this: champagne gold `#DAB25E`. Use it harder.

### 4. Big-name client logos above the fold — *quality over quantity*
Tier 11 shows 6 logos and looks more premium than Disruptive's 16. **The premium move is fewer, better logos.**

### 5. Methodology has a proper noun
"Prophit Engine" (CTC), "$100M Money Models" (ACQ), "Authenticity Wins™" (DA), "Marketing Plan" (KB). **Productize the framework. Trademark-style naming.** CFS does NOT have this — biggest single opportunity.

## What This Means for CFS

The competitive gap is wide open in one specific direction:

> **"Premium, dark, restrained, with a named methodology — for operator-CEOs."**

- The lit-up dark/gold aesthetic CFS already owns is closer to **Acquisition.com** than anyone, but without the info-product baggage.
- Tier 11's "diagnostic first" tone is the right voice — but they look corporate.
- Nobody owns **dark + editorial + champagne**. That gap is CFS's.
- The single biggest move: **name the system**. The 6-step process needs a proper noun.

Working names to consider for the CFS methodology:
- "The Flow System™"
- "The Profitable Spend Engine™"
- "The CEO Flow Method™"
- "The Profit Infrastructure System™"
- "The Operator Engine™"

Recommended: **"The CEO Flow Method™"** (or **"The Flow System™"** as a tighter short-form). Brand-aligned, operator-coded, ownable.
