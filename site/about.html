Scaled a consulting business from zero to $1.2M per month.

Over the last several years I've managed more than $20M in paid acquisition spend across coaching, staffing, construction, roofing, and B2B services. What every account had in common, at the start, wasn't an ads problem. It was an infrastructure problem dressed up as one.

The tracking was leaking. The attribution was wrong. The CRM didn't know what the ads knew. Closers were taking calls cold. Creative cadence was running on instinct instead of cycles. The ads weren't broken. The system underneath was.

CEO Flow Systems is built to fix that, for the operator-CEOs already past the messy middle, already spending real money, already running real sales teams. We don't take everyone. We don't run percentage-of-spend. We work like senior operators, because that's what the work requires.

Philosophy

What I'm obsessed with.

Most ad accounts I've audited have one thing in common. The dashboard says "lower CPL" and the operator says "I still can't tell which ads make me money." That gap is where the work lives.

I'm obsessed with closing it. Attribution that survives iOS updates and Safari's privacy posture. CRM that knows what the platform knew when the lead came in. A closer who picks up the phone with the buyer's intent already on the screen. Creative cadence that runs on a calendar, not a panic.

None of that is glamorous. It's the part most agencies skip because it doesn't make a good case study screenshot. It's also the part that turns ad spend from an expense line into a compounding asset, and that's the only outcome worth selling.

What I look for

  • An offer that's already converting.
  • A sales process that closes.
  • An operator who treats the engagement as a partnership, not a vendor relationship.
  • A willingness to look honestly at what's broken.

What I won't take

  • Companies hoping ads will fix a broken sales engine.
  • Operators looking for a percentage-of-spend partner.
  • Anyone who needs the work to start before week three.
  • Engagements I can't reasonably make compound in 90 days.
Operating principles

How we work, three commitments, no exceptions.

01

Senior operators only.

The person who diagnoses your account is the person who runs it. No junior handoffs, no offshore relay, no account-manager middleman. Headcount stays deliberately small so the people doing the work are the people you spoke to on the call.

02

Transparency is structural.

You see what we see. One dashboard. Real-time attribution. Spend, pipeline, and close rate surfaced together. No screenshot Fridays.

03

Engagements are scoped to compound.

We don't take engagements that can't reasonably compound inside ninety days of instrumented infrastructure. If we can't build something durable, we say so on the call.

The work is the system underneath.
